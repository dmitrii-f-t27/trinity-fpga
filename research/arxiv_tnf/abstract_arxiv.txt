A ternary node has exactly three sites that could carry a number format, and only
one needs to. The weight is a code: multiplying by it is a sign-select, not a
multiply. The sample arrives ADC-native. The accumulator is the only object left
with a range to spend, and Ternary Network Floats (TNF) are what spends it -- a
sign, a balanced-ternary exponent of E_t trits, an M-bit mantissa, with
1+E_t+M=N.

We close both halves. GFTernary, a two-bit {-phi,0,+phi} alphabet,
carries the weight, and phi is forced: it is the unique r>1 with
r^2=r+1, which returns the product of two weights to the lattice the datapath
already adds in and makes the gain of k layers exactly F_kphi+F_{k-1}, so
depth never reintroduces a multiplier. On an XC7A200T in a fully open flow the
pair costs zero DSP across the stack: a matched filter demodulating real air at
78 MHz, every transformer block, a systolic GEMM at 33 GOPS, a trained
ternary transformer at 97%, and on-silicon backpropagation training XOR to
4/4.

Among formats carrying their own range, ours are first and second on throughput
per area: GFTernary 0.177 and TNF4 0.167 MHz/LUT at 2 and 4 bits, against
binary32's 0.163 and posit32's 0.030 -- 5.9x our first, at 16x
the storage.

The ladder is a frontier. A width-N format of effective mantissa m and range
b binades is dominated exactly when m+log_3(b+1)<= N-1, while any uniform
format obeys m+log_2(b+1)<= N-1: the same books in two currencies. Every
uniform binary format is dominated with slack 0.3691E positions; of 28
catalogued formats from 8 to 128 bits none escapes, and Ternary27, the only
ternary-radix competitor, falls with five positions to spare.

The accumulator obeys a law we derive and then measure: error grows as
sqrt(pK) 2^{-(M+1)} in fan-in, fitted at +0.476 against a predicted +0.5.
Eleven of our own claims were falsified by the measurements meant to support them,
and are retracted in place.
