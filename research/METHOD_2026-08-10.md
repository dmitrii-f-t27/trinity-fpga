# What twenty-two iterations produced

Fifteen withdrawals, two of them subsequently withdrawn in turn, three classes of
harness confound, six theorems about measurement, and two gates in CI. The method
turned out to be worth more than any number it produced, so it is written down
here rather than left as a trail of commits.

---

## 1. The register: which number rests on which instrument

A claim is only as good as the rig under it. This is the current state.

| claim | instrument | status |
|---|---|---|

| Decoder cost: fixed fields separate from tapers **on frequency**, 1.43x worst-to-best | post-route, median of 5, full observation | **clean** |

| Ladder accuracy, `M_eff` by rung | oracle, exact rationals | clean, but see §4 |
| Frontier domination | positions, not bits | **withdrawn** |

## 2. The withdrawals, by cause

Every one had a correct measurement inside a wrong comparison.

**Wrong baseline** (1). Compared the `phi` scale grid against powers of two when
the field deploys APoT. Ours loses by 15x on the real baseline.

**Wrong units** (8, 9). The frontier condition counted positions; formats are
stored in bits, and `log2(3) log3(x) = log2(x)` makes the slack vanish exactly.
The ladder's own rungs are 25-60% wider than their names.

**Wrong regime** (3, 4, 5). Area comparisons made at a five-bit shift field when
the workload spans two; with a frozen scale, at a runtime one; with runtime
composition, at compile-time depth.

**Wrong artefact quoted** (6). Cited a format decoder as the cost of an LNS
adder, off by two orders of magnitude.

**Divergent artefacts** (10, 11). Oracle and RTL implemented different formats
under one name; the synthesised one had no catalogue row at all.

**Wrong instrument** (12). Declared `nextpnr-xilinx` absent by checking `PATH`
while the repository's own script pointed at it.

**Harness confounds** (13, 14, 15). Three separate classes, below.

## 3. The three harness confounds

None was visible in any number. Every design synthesised, routed and reported
plausible figures at every stage.

**A shared component is a confound, not a constant.** An identical block in every
candidate leaves area differences intact and does **not** leave delay differences
intact, because it may hold the critical path. Removing a common accumulator
changed the area-frequency correlation from **−0.90 to −0.62** and reversed a
group-separation conclusion.

**Partial observation is an unequal pruner.** Synthesis removes logic not
reaching an output, so a harness observing a fixed number of bits cuts each
design in proportion to its **own** output width. Observing four bits of a 32-bit
datapath left 27 LUT of 179; an adjacent 6-bit design was barely touched. The
comparison ran one design at 15% of itself against another nearly whole.

**Harness subtraction requires harness invariance.** A design consuming fewer
harness sources prunes the rest, so the subtrahend varies with the design.
Measured: `apot_requant` appeared to be 15 LUT because three of four source LFSRs
had been removed from its build.

**The fix, in one sentence:** fold every output of the design under test, and
every source the harness provides, into a single observed reduction.

## 4. Six theorems about measurement

**T1 (comparison terms are part of the result).** A ratio is meaningful only if
both sides were built as their own advocate would build them. Fifteen times a
ratio changed sign or magnitude when the other side was rebuilt properly.

**T2 (a ranking needs a resolution).** A ranking on a metric with spread `s`
asserts an ordering only between rows separated by more than `s`. Three of the
top four gaps in the silicon table were smaller than the spread.

**T3 (area is deterministic, timing is not).** Under placement-seed variation LUT
count is invariant and frequency moves 11.4%. Any metric combining them inherits
the timing variance in full.

**T4 (a ratio over correlated factors restates the dominant one).** If `F(A)`
decreases in `A`, a ranking on `F/A` approximates the ranking on `1/A`. Measured
at Spearman 0.961 -- "throughput per area" was mostly restating area.

**T5 (a monotone response to instrument improvement is a warning).** If
successive corrections all move a result the same way, the remaining confounds
probably do too. The LNS advantage read 8.6x, 14x, 11.5x, 5.5x, 4.4x across five
instruments. It is not converged.

**T6 (a claim's sign can converge while its magnitude does not).** An ordering
established across several instruments is stronger evidence than a magnitude
measured once on the best of them. Report them separately.

## 5. Rules adopted

- Before reporting a ratio, write down what the competitor would build **if it
  were trying to win**, and build that.
- A correction that invalidates a comparison applies to **every** claim resting
  on the same quantity, not only where it surfaced. Applied once, this produced
  the two deepest findings of the night on consecutive iterations.
- An instrument documented as unreliable must not be used again for the same
  class of claim.
- An absent tool is a claim about the **machine**; `PATH` is not the machine.
  Read the repository's own build scripts before recording a ceiling.
- Report LUT from one run and frequency as a median over at least five seeds,
  with the spread stated. Do not rank rows separated by less than the spread.
- A competitor's cardinality is a **specification fact**, asserted against the
  spec in the harness, never derived from a shared helper.
- Check a claim you believe is wrong even when it turns out right -- iteration 14
  set out to disprove a table, the table survived, and two unrelated defects
  surfaced.

## 6. The gates, and why each exists

**`conformance/check_artefact_agreement.py`** -- compares declared parameters
**across** artefacts. Every other check verifies an artefact against itself, and
all of them passed while the oracle and the RTL implemented different formats
under one name. 13 known, ratcheted.

**`tools/check_script_rot.py`** -- every module a build script instantiates must
exist in the files it reads. The script that produced the silicon table had been
broken by a rename for an unknown period; nobody noticed because the table was
already written. *A reproduction path that is never run is a claim, not a
capability.*

**`tools/check_harness.py`** -- every output of the design under test must reach
the observed port. 83 known, ratcheted. It caught its author's own correction one
hour after that correction was written.

**All fourteen fixed fields sit above all three tapers on frequency.** The worst
fixed leads the best taper by **1.43x**, outside every relevant spread. On area
the two overlap: posit8 at 214 LUT is smaller than IBM hex32 at 243.

That asymmetry is the result, not an inconvenience. A taper's decode is a scan --
a serial dependency whose length grows with the word -- and a serial dependency
costs critical path directly and area only incidentally. A radix-heavy fixed
format costs a wide shift instead: area-heavy and shallow. The families therefore
separate on delay and not on area, and the overlap is predicted rather than
excused.

**GFTernary against posit32: 7.8x on area and 19.9x on frequency.**

**GFTernary costs 66 LUT where a bare wire costs 112.** Decoding a two-bit
alphabet lets the synthesiser simplify the downstream register further than
passing 32 raw bits does.

Area-frequency correlation on the isolated decoder: **−0.60**, against −0.90 on
the combined harness. The two axes carry independent information here, which is
why they are reported separately and no ratio of them appears in this table.

## 9. The number that had no value

The LNS advantage was chased across six instruments: 8.6x, 14x, 11.5x, 5.5x,
4.4x, 5.8x. The first five moved it down and the sixth moved it up, because that
one was not a confound removal but a change of which property is held equal.

`Z[phi]` has range linear in width and exact precision; LNS has range exponential
in its integer field and relative precision. Matching bits favours us at 5.8x;
matching range would need 256 bits of `Z[phi]` state against LNS-32's 32 and we
would lose; matching precision is impossible in the other direction.

**T7 (incommensurability at equal storage).** Systems whose range scales
differently in width, and whose precision is absolute in one case and relative in
the other, have no storage-matched area ratio. The value depends on an unstated
choice, so it has no limit and cannot converge.

Six instrument corrections were made in pursuit of it. Every one was a real
defect worth fixing, and three of them now have gates. None could have produced
convergence.

**Before chasing a number's stability, check that the comparison producing it is**
**well-posed.**
