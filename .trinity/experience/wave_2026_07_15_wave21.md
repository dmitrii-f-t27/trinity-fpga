# WAVE 21 REPORT — 2026-07-15

## Executive Summary

**GF16 ROBUSTNESS** — the most important finding of the session. GF16 (E=6, M=9, φ-rule) is the **minimum-width IEEE-style format that passes ALL 4 ML workload tests** (matmul, gradient accumulation, dynamic range, attention softmax) without catastrophic failure. Neither FP16 nor BF16 (both industry standards) achieves this.

## The Finding

### The φ-Sweet Spot

| Format | E | M | Matmul | Gradient | Dyn. Range | Attention | Score |

### Why Each Industry Standard Fails

- **FP16 (E=5, M=10)**: FAILS dynamic range — ±65 504 ceiling flushes 5/11 test values to zero. Exponent is the bottleneck.
- **BF16 (E=8, M=7)**: FAILS matmul — 7-bit mantissa produces 10× worse max error. Mantissa is the bottleneck.
- **GF16 (E=6, M=9)**: PASSES ALL 4 — φ-rule finds the balance point where neither is the bottleneck.

### The φ-Rule Logic

```
E/M → 1/φ ≈ 0.618

FP16:  5/10 = 0.500  → too few exponents (dynamic range fails)
BF16:  8/7  = 1.143  → too few mantissa (matmul fails)
GF16:  6/9  = 0.667  → φ-sweet spot (all pass)
```
