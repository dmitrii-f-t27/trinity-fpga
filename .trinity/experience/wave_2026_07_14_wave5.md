## KEY FINDING: LUT Comparison Honest Measurement

### The false claim (Wave 4)
"GF16: 118 LUT, 4-11x lower than tekum (480) and takum (1350)"

### The measured reality (Wave 5)

| Module | LUT | Method |
|--------|-----|--------|
| GF16 (gf_adder_param, current) | **486** | yosys 0.63 -abc9 -nocarry |
| GF16 (gf16_add_top, old) | **176** | same (BENCH-005 said 118 — stale) |
| tekum16 (stub, 65% bit-exact) | **573** | same |
| takum16 | **N/A** | RTL adder does NOT EXIST |

**Ratio**: GF16-param / tekum16-stub = 0.85x (15% smaller, not 4-11x)

### Why it was wrong
1. "118 LUT" was from BENCH-005 (May 2026) — the JSON artifact is MISSING, same module now gives 176
2. The 118 was for the OLD non-parameterized adder, not the current gf_adder_param
3. tekum16 "480" was an estimate, never synthesized
4. takum16 "1350" was from literature (different FPGA, different toolchain)
5. **takum16 adder RTL does not exist** — the comparison was imaginary

### Corrected claim
> GF16 (486 LUT) and tekum16 (573 LUT) occupy different points on the
> area-vs-dynamic-range trade-off. Neither dominates.
