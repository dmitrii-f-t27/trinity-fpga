## WAVE A: Pipeline/Clamp — Partial Success

### Barrel Shifter Clamp
- **Change**: `ediff` clamped to `MANT_BITS+4` (43 for GF64), reducing barrel shifter from 25 to 6 mux levels

- **Commodity FPGA today**: GF16 (proven silicon, lowest LUT, competitive accuracy)

- **Catalog paper**: all three in the benchmark — breadth is the moat
