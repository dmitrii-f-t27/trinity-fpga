## 6. Skills saved (durable lessons for future fires)

- **Formal proof of a clocked FP unit**: don't fight the reset/init-state artifact of a sequential harness. Expose the combinational core via an `` `ifdef FORMAL``-guarded observation tap, hold clk=0/rst=0, and prove `mismatch == 0` with `yosys sat -prove`. Pure-combinational → no init artifact → real proof in seconds. Oracle must implement the FULL spec (incl. IEEE +0+(−0)=+0 zero-sign rule) or the miter correctly flags it.
- **openXC7/DSP**: `synth_xilinx -nodsp` is mandatory — nextpnr-xilinx can't route DSP48E1; force LUT multiply.
- **nextpnr placer**: wide mantissa (MANT_BITS≥12) needs `--placer heap`; `sa` fails 6+ seeds. Drop `--freq` too.
- **AX7203 flash**: passwordless-`sudo openocd` (`pld load 0 <bit>; runtest 200000; shutdown`), IDCODE `0x13636093`, UART CP2102N `/dev/cu.usbserial-120` @160000.

encoding ≠ compute ≠ FPGA. Formal proof [доказано, GF4/6/8/12]; HW cells [измерено на железе, Tier E]; structural SW [требует t27].
